Operating principles

Built as infrastructure,
not a collection of pages.

01

Source-grounded

Primary authorities remain visible and control official definitions.

02

Explicit boundaries

Coverage, assumptions and unresolved gaps are stated rather than hidden.

03

Modular by design

New datasets and analytical products can join without breaking existing tools.

04

Open and portable

Static hosting and downloadable formats keep the system accessible and reusable.

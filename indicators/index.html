Coverage statement

Source-complete within a declared boundary.

This release captures every eligible catalogue record from WHO IMR, World Bank Health, the UN Global SDG framework and UNICEF’s global indicator codelist, plus the 80-item priority collection. It remains incomplete across specialist, regional and national authorities listed in the source census.

Programme 1 · NTEP

A community-to-national tuberculosis indicator vertical.

The NTEP deep release traces official KPIs, case-finding and diagnostic measures, CBNAAT and Truenat monthly reporting elements, laboratory abstracts and quality indicators, drug-resistant TB monitoring, and TB Mukt Panchayat verification measures. Each record identifies its lowest reporting level, facility or community unit, responsible cadre, lineage and exact official source location.
